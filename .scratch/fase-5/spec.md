## Problem Statement

Today every approved house is «Activa» forever. The launch offer exists only as a counter of active houses («Quedan N de 50») and a fixed line «Gratis 12 meses · oferta de lanzamiento» on every card: no house knows which tier it got or when its free period ends. Nothing warns the owner before the offer ends, nothing hides the house when it ends without a payment method, there is no payment method at all, and the owner cannot stop or resume a subscription. The schema from `0001` has `subscriptions`, `subscription_reminders`, `subscription_state()` and the `payment_due`/`inactive` statuses, but nothing writes them; the columns do not fit the outbox (a reminder row is «sent» on insert) and the card token lives per house although the design shows one payment method per account.

## Solution

- **Offer slot at first approval.** The first time bnbtico approves a house it takes the next launch slot (1–50 → 12 months, 51–100 → 6, 101–200 → 3; afterwards no free period) and its free period starts that day: «Gratis hasta el 14 oct 2027». The slot is kept for life (editing, stopping, resuming, re-approval never take a new one). The counter «Quedan N de 50» counts assigned slots, not active houses.
- **Lifecycle** (every transition is an RPC): En revisión → (approved) Activa with offer, or Pendiente de pago without offer and without method → at the end of the offer: Activa (paid plan, no charge yet) if the owner has a payment method, otherwise Pendiente de pago → adding a method turns every Pendiente de pago house of that owner into Activa → «Parar suscripción» makes it Inactiva → «Reactivar» returns it to Activa or Pendiente de pago by the same rule. The offer keeps running while a house is stopped or being edited (prototype; Daniel 2026-10-27).
- **Blocked link.** Pendiente de pago and Inactiva houses leave `listings_public`, so they disappear from Explorar, the sitemap, Favoritos and contact; their link shows the existing 410 «Este enlace ya no está activo» with the reason. The existing purge trigger refreshes the cached page on every status change.
- **Reminders 90/30/7/0** before the end of the offer, only while the house has no payment method: a card in the app during the whole 90-day window and one WhatsApp per threshold with the link `…/propietario/suscripcion#pago=CT-####`. Outbox: `pg_cron` enqueues in `subscription_reminders`; the worker's cron sends through a `WhatsAppSender` (fake in local/CI, Twilio WhatsApp sandbox in pruebas) and marks the row. A reminder is never sent twice.
- **Payment method with Stripe in test mode, nothing charged.** «Añadir método de pago» asks the worker for a Stripe Checkout session in *setup* mode (saves the card, no charge), the owner fills the card on Stripe's page and comes back; Stripe's signed webhook → `payment_events(provider, event_id)` unique → RPC `apply_payment_event`. We store only Stripe's opaque ids and brand/last 4/expiry. Behind a `PaymentProvider` interface with a fake for local, CI and Playwright. The gateway for real charges is decided later (company in Costa Rica pending); Ana's personal EUR Stripe account is for test mode only.
- **Screens:** «Promoción y suscripción» (`/propietario/suscripcion`, reached from Cuenta, Inicio and the `#pago=` link), the Suscripción card and «Método de pago» row in Cuenta (design 20), real offer data in Inicio (design 9), states and the pay button in Mis casas (10) and the house detail (19), and the admin Casas list (23).
- **Test clock without a production door:** a service-only RPC moves **one house's** offer end so it has N days left; a worker route in pruebas/local calls it and runs the daily jobs and the sender at once. Locked twice: the worker answers 404 when `ENVIRONMENT` is `production`, and the SQL refuses unless a Vault secret that only exists in pruebas and local is present.

## User Stories

### Owner

1. As an owner whose house was just approved, I want to see which launch tier it got and until when it is free, so that I know when I will start paying.
2. As an owner, I want the counter «Quedan N de 50» to count real places, so that I trust the offer.
3. As an owner, I want my house to keep its free period if I edit it or stop it, and not to restart it, so that the rules are clear (and nobody games them).
4. As an owner without a payment method, I want a notice in the app from 90 days before the end and a WhatsApp at 90, 30 and 7 days and on the last day, each with a link straight to the payment screen, so that my house does not disappear by surprise.
5. As an owner, I want «Añadir método de pago» only when it matters (no method and inside the notice window, or the house is Pendiente de pago), so that the app does not push me to give a card a year early.
6. As an owner, I want to add my card once for all my houses, on a page that I recognise as secure, and to be told that nothing is charged now, so that I feel safe.
7. As an owner whose offer ended without a method, I want the house hidden and its link to say it is not active, and I want it back the moment I add a method, so that I control when it shows.
8. As an owner, I want to stop the subscription of one house (Inactiva, hidden, nothing charged) and resume it later with the same link, so that I am not tied («sin permanencia»).
9. As an owner opening the `#pago=CT-####` link from WhatsApp without a session, I want to sign in and land on that house in «Promoción y suscripción», so that the link always works.
10. As an owner, I want Cuenta to tell me each house's situation, my payment method and the next charge, so that I see my bill at a glance.

### Client

11. As a client opening a link of a stopped or unpaid house, I want «Este enlace ya no está activo» with the reason and a way to other houses, never a broken page, so that I understand what happened.
12. As a client, I never want to see in Explorar, Favoritos or the sitemap a house I cannot contact.

### bnbtico (operator)

13. As the operator, I want every state change to go through one database function, whoever calls it (browser, worker, `pg_cron`), so that the rules hold.
14. As the operator, I want each reminder sent exactly once even if the cron retries or two runs overlap, so that owners are not spammed.
15. As the operator, I want no card data in our database, worker or logs, and webhooks accepted only with a valid signature and only once, so that money handling is safe.
16. As the operator, I want to make a house go through every state in minutes in pruebas, and no way to do that in production.
17. As the admin, I want the Casas list with Activas / Por pagar / Inactivas and each house's subscription line, so that I can answer owners.

### Domain (`src/domain/subscription.ts`, no framework; the Vitest port of the prototype rules)

- Constants: `LAUNCH_TIERS` (moved/reused from `offer.ts`), `MONTHLY_FEE_CRC`, `REMINDER_DAYS = [90, 30, 7, 0]`.
- `tierForSlot(slot)` → `{ months } | null` (1–50 → 12, 51–100 → 6, 101–200 → 3, > 200 → null); `launchOffer(taken)` keeps its shape but its input is now «slots assigned».
- `promoEndsOn(approvalDay, months)`: same day number N months later, clamped to the month's last day (31 ene + 1 → 28/29 feb); the end date is the **last free day**.
- `daysLeft(endsOn, today)` (Costa Rica dates, `costaRicaToday`); `reminderThreshold(daysLeft)` = the smallest threshold ≥ daysLeft within the window (91 → null, 90…31 → 90, 30…8 → 30, 7…1 → 7, 0 → 0, < 0 → null). This fixes the prototype bug (`find` always returned 90). Catch-up rule: if the job missed a day, the house gets the threshold it is in now; earlier missed thresholds are not sent.
- `nextStatus(current, event, ctx)` — the pure state machine shared by the fake and the optimistic updates, mirroring the SQL: events `approve`, `dayPassed`, `methodAdded`, `stop`, `resume`; ctx `{ promoRunning, hasMethod }`. Rules: approve → `active` if promo running or method, else `payment_due`; offer ends → `active` (plan paid) with method, `payment_due` without; method added → `payment_due` → `active`; stop allowed from `active` and `payment_due` → `inactive`; resume from `inactive` → by the approve rule. Any other (state, event) pair is refused with a Spanish message (`SUBSCRIPTION_MESSAGES`, identical in SQL and fake).
- `payButtonDue(sub)` = no method **and** (status `payment_due` **or** status `active` within the 90-day window) — the `mis-casas.js` rule, applied in **every** view (the prototype's `o-sub` ignored it; that was a contradiction).
- Copy builders (usted): `subscriptionLine(sub, today)` for cards and Cuenta («Gratis hasta el 14 oct 2027», «Pendiente de pago», «Suscripción activa desde el 15 oct 2027 · ₡2 900 al mes», «Parada el 9 oct · no se cobra», «En revisión · ₡0»); `reminderCard(daysLeft)` («Su oferta gratuita termina en 30 días» / «…termina mañana» / «…termina hoy», body «No se le cobrará nada hasta entonces. Añada un método de pago antes de esa fecha para que su casa siga saliendo en bnbtico; después, ₡2 900 al mes por casa, sin permanencia. También se lo recordamos por WhatsApp.»); `reminderWhatsApp({ name, title, endsOn, link, daysBefore })` — 90/30/7: «Hola, <nombre>. Somos bnbtico. La oferta gratuita de «<casa>» termina el <fecha>: hasta entonces no se le cobra nada. Para que su casa siga saliendo en bnbtico después, añada un método de pago aquí: <enlace> Desde esa fecha, ₡2 900 al mes por casa, sin permanencia; puede pararla cuando quiera.»; 0: «… La oferta gratuita de «<casa>» termina hoy. Si no añade un método de pago, desde mañana su casa deja de mostrarse en bnbtico: <enlace> …». `payLink(base, publicId)` = `<base>/propietario/suscripcion#pago=CT-####`; `parsePayHash(hash)` accepts only `^#pago=CT-\d{4,}$`.
- `nextCharge(houses, method, today)` → «₡0 · sin método de pago» / «₡0 · ninguna casa activa» / «₡2 900 · 15 oct 2027» (sum of the active houses whose paid period starts first; shown as information, nothing is charged in v1).
- `blockedReason(reason)` → the three 410 texts (below).
- **Rules from the prototype tests that apply to v1 and must be Vitest cases** (`estados-casa.cjs`, `mis-casas.cjs`, `house-controls.cjs`; fake timers instead of the real `Date.now()`): a house inside its offer is Activa and has a reminder when ≤ 90 days remain; one with > 90 days has none; the reminder card text and «También se lo recordamos por WhatsApp» appear and the raw WhatsApp text never does; offer end without method → Pendiente de pago, not public, out of Explorar; adding a method → Activa, «Suscripción activa», public; method saved before the end → at the end the plan is paid and the house stays Activa; stop → Inactiva, not public, no contact; resuming needs no identity check (Veriff is v1.1) — only an owner account that is active; the 410 for a stopped house says «paró la suscripción» and has no contact button; for an unpaid one «regularice su suscripción»; after resuming the contact button is back; the WhatsApp text says nothing is charged until that date, carries `#pago=CT-####` and does not mention «Cuenta → Promoción»; the pay button shows for the house inside the window and not for the one outside, and disappears once a method exists; stopping a house leaves the others untouched; a house you do not own cannot be stopped/resumed. Not ported: Veriff, direct contact, the «Simular fin de oferta · demo» button (replaced by the test clock), web-only layout assertions, the prototype's `localStorage` persistence.

### Schema (one migration `fase5_suscripcion`; `0001` not edited)

- **`subscriptions`** (empty everywhere, so reshaped in place): add `promo_slot int unique check (promo_slot between 1 and 200)`, `promo_ends_on date`, `paid_since date`; drop `promo_ends_at`, `payment_method_token`, `payment_method_added_at`; drop `subscription_state()` (replaced by RPC logic). `plan` stays (`promo` while the offer runs or `paid`). Revoke all from anon/authenticated; select policy for owner/admin stays.
- **`payment_methods`** (one per owner): `owner_id pk → profiles`, `provider text check in ('stripe','fake')`, `customer_ref text`, `method_ref text`, `brand text`, `last4 char(4)`, `exp_month int`, `exp_year int`, `added_at`, `updated_at`. RLS owner/admin select; **column grant** `select (owner_id, provider, brand, last4, exp_month, exp_year, added_at)` to authenticated — the refs are never readable from the browser. No writes for anon/authenticated.
- **`payment_customers`** `(owner_id, provider) pk, customer_ref` — the Stripe customer created before the card exists. Service only.
- **`payment_events`** `(provider, event_id) unique, type, owner_id, received_at, result text` — no payload stored. Service only.
- **`subscription_reminders`** (empty, reshaped): keep `pk (listing_id, days_before)` (it is what makes «never enqueued twice» structural); add `promo_ends_on date`, `queued_at`, `status text check in ('queued','sending','sent','failed','unknown','skipped')`, `attempts int`, `claimed_at`, `to_phone`, `body`, `provider_ref`, `last_error`; `sent_at` becomes nullable without default. Owner/admin select stays (Promoción y suscripción shows «Aviso por WhatsApp enviado el …»); no writes for anon/authenticated.
- **Backfill:** active houses without a subscription get slots in `first_published_at` order and `promo_ends_on = promoEndsOn(first_published_at CR day, months)`. Inserts only into `subscriptions` (no `listings` update, so no `app.allow_status` needed); tested on a local base that already has active houses (not just after `db reset`).
- **RPCs** (`security definer`, `search_path = ''`, Spanish messages identical to `SUBSCRIPTION_MESSAGES`; all status writes set `app.allow_status`):
  - `review_listing` (replace): on approve, if the house has no subscription, take the next slot under `pg_advisory_xact_lock` (two simultaneous approvals never get the same slot; slot > 200 ⇒ `plan = 'paid'`, no promo); status by the approve rule (promo running or owner has a method ⇒ `active`, else `payment_due`). Re-approval after editing reuses the subscription.
  - `owner_billing()` (authenticated) → JSON `{ houses: [{ listing_id, public_id, title, status, plan, promo_slot, promo_months, promo_ends_on, paid_since, stopped_at, monthly_price_crc, reminders: [{ days_before, status, sent_at }] }], method: { brand, last4, exp_month, exp_year, added_at } | null, today }` for the session's own houses that have a subscription. One request for Cuenta, Inicio, Mis casas and the subscription screen.
  - `stop_subscription(p_listing)`, `resume_subscription(p_listing)` (authenticated, own house, account active) → new status.
  - `launch_offer_taken()` (anon, authenticated) → count of assigned slots.
  - `listing_blocked_reason(p_public_id)` (anon, authenticated) → `'stopped' | 'payment' | 'other' | null`. A separate function so `listing_link_state` keeps its values (the old app in `apps/web` still calls it).
  - `run_subscription_day(p_today date default today CR)` (service only): (1) expire: active promo houses with `today > promo_ends_on` → with method `plan = 'paid'`, `paid_since = promo_ends_on + 1`, stay active; without → `payment_due`; (2) enqueue: active promo houses without method whose `reminderThreshold` is not null → `insert … on conflict do nothing` with `status 'queued'`. Returns counts. Job `bnbtico-suscripciones` at `5 6 * * *` (00:05 Costa Rica, after the reopening job).
  - `claim_reminders(p_limit int)` (service only): `for update skip locked` on `queued` rows (oldest first), sets `status 'sending'`, `claimed_at`, `attempts + 1`, `to_phone` (owner's current phone), `body` (built in SQL from the same template as the domain — or built by the worker with `reminderWhatsApp` and stored by `finish_reminders`; the ticket picks one and tests both sides give the same text); returns what the sender needs. Rows of houses that are no longer active promo without method are marked `skipped` instead of returned (the owner added a card or stopped the house meanwhile).
  - `finish_reminders(p_results jsonb)` (service only): per id `sent` (sets `sent_at`, `provider_ref`), `retry` (definite refusal by the provider before acceptance: back to `queued` if attempts < 5, else `failed`), `unknown` (no answer / timeout: **never retried automatically**, kept for an alert), `skipped`. A `sending` row is never reclaimed by `claim_reminders`.
  - `payment_setup_context()` (authenticated) → `{ owner_id, full_name, phone, customer_ref }` for the session's account (refuses deactivated accounts) — the worker calls it **with the user's JWT**, so PostgREST validates the session (no JWT verification in the worker).
  - `save_payment_customer(p_owner, p_provider, p_ref)` and `apply_payment_event(p_provider, p_event_id, p_type, p_owner, p_customer_ref, p_method_ref, p_brand, p_last4, p_exp_month, p_exp_year)` (service only): insert into `payment_events` → on conflict return `'duplicate'` and do nothing; unknown types → `'ignored'` (recorded); setup completed → upsert `payment_methods`, then every `payment_due` house of that owner → `active`, `plan = 'paid'`, `paid_since = today`; promo houses stay promo. Returns `'applied'`.
  - `test_set_offer_days_left(p_listing, p_days_left int)` (service only; refuses unless Vault has `bnbtico_test_clock = 'on'`): sets `promo_ends_on = today + p_days_left` (negative allowed, so the next `run_subscription_day` expires it). It never touches status itself.
- DB tests: direct writes on the four tables → 42501 for owner and anon; refs not selectable; service-only RPCs → 42501 for anon/authenticated; job registered; concurrent approvals get different slots; backfill; the outbox never re-enqueues (pk) and `claim_reminders` from two sessions at once returns disjoint rows; `sending` never reclaimed; `test_set_offer_days_left` refused without the Vault secret; status changes enqueue one purge each (Vault secrets in a rolled-back transaction, as in `admin-aal2-purga.test.ts`).

### Worker (`src/server`; new modules `billing.ts`, `reminders.ts`, `test-clock.ts`; dependencies injected: `PaymentProvider`, `WhatsAppSender`, clock, service-role data)

- **Interfaces** in the domain (no framework): `PaymentProvider { createSetupSession({ owner, customerRef, successUrl, cancelUrl }) → { url, customerRef }; verifyWebhook(request) → PaymentEvent | null }` and `WhatsAppSender { send(to, body) → { ok: true, ref } | { ok: false, definite: boolean, error } }`. Implementations: `StripeProvider` (REST with `fetch`, no SDK: `POST /v1/customers`, `POST /v1/checkout/sessions` with `mode=setup`, `GET /v1/setup_intents/:id?expand[]=payment_method` for brand/last 4; webhook signature `Stripe-Signature` HMAC-SHA256 with Web Crypto, 5-minute tolerance, constant-time compare), `FakeProvider` (local/CI/Playwright: the «checkout» URL is a worker page `/_pago-falso/:session` with «Guardar tarjeta de prueba» that posts a signed fake event to the webhook and redirects back; `ENVIRONMENT=local` only), `TwilioSender` (`POST /2010-04-01/Accounts/:sid/Messages.json`, `From=whatsapp:<sandbox>`, `To=whatsapp:<E.164>`; 4xx/5xx = definite, network error/timeout = unknown), `FakeSender` (records in memory / logs; in local it marks rows sent with `provider_ref 'fake-…'`). An **allowlist** `WHATSAPP_ALLOWLIST` (E.164 list, pruebas only): numbers outside it are `skipped`, so the test project never writes to a real person by mistake.
- **Routes:** `POST /api/pago/sesion` (body `{ publicId? }`, `Authorization: Bearer <user JWT>`): `payment_setup_context` with that JWT → reuse or create the customer (`save_payment_customer`) → session URL (success `<base>/propietario/suscripcion?pago=ok#pago=CT-####`, cancel without `pago=ok`); 401 without a valid session, 403 deactivated account, 503 without payments configured. `POST /webhooks/stripe` (and `/webhooks/fake` in local): verify signature → 400 if invalid (nothing written) → for `checkout.session.completed` with `mode=setup`: retrieve the SetupIntent → `apply_payment_event` → 200 (also on `duplicate`; 500 only if the database fails, so Stripe retries). `POST /_pruebas/oferta/CT-####` (body `{ diasRestantes }`, `Bearer TEST_CLOCK_SECRET`): **404 when `ENVIRONMENT=production`** or without the secret; `test_set_offer_days_left` → `run_subscription_day` → one send batch → JSON with what happened. All added to `run_worker_first`.
- **Cron:** one trigger per environment as today but **hourly** (`17 * * * *`), because the free plan allows 5 cron triggers per account and three are used: `runDaily` keeps keep-alive/prune/FX at the 09 UTC run; `sendReminders` runs only between 08:00 and 17:59 Costa Rica (14–23 UTC) so nobody gets a WhatsApp at 3 a.m.; `landingOnly` skips everything as today. A batch is ≤ 20 reminders = 1 claim + ≤ 20 sends + 1 finish ≤ 22 subrequests (free limit 50) and a few ms of CPU; leftover rows go in the next hour.
- **Secrets / vars:** `PAYMENTS` (`stripe` | `fake` | unset = off), `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `WHATSAPP_SENDER` (`twilio` | `fake` | unset = off), `TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID`, `TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN`, `TWILIO_WHATSAPP_FROM`, `WHATSAPP_ALLOWLIST`, `TEST_CLOCK_SECRET`. The worker refuses a `sk_live_` key outside production and, in this phase, refuses payments in production entirely (real charges are out). `pnpm dev:vars` writes the fake ones.
- **Money safety:** no card field ever renders on bnbtico; the worker only sees Stripe ids, brand, last 4 and expiry; logs never include request bodies of the webhook or Stripe responses (only event id, type, result). The Stripe page is a top-level navigation, so the CSP of the shell does not change (no third-party script, no `connect-src` to Stripe). If a later gateway needs its script on our page, the exception is limited to that route and justified in `decisiones.md`.
